Who we are

Soumya Chauhan Permanent Makeup Studio, Chitrwani Road, Purnea, Bihar. Contact: +91 62079 34584.

What we collect

Only what you send us: your name, phone number, chosen service, preferred date and any notes you add in the booking form or on WhatsApp. We do not run user accounts or take online payments on this website.

How we use it

To reply to you, confirm appointments and keep you informed about your treatment. We do not sell your details.

Health and consent

Before permanent makeup we may ask about your health, allergies and medicines. This is kept private and used only to decide if treatment is safe for you.

Photos and videos

We post before and after photos or videos only with your written permission. You can ask us to remove them at any time.

Third-party services

This site uses Google Fonts and is hosted on Vercel. Booking messages open in WhatsApp, and any Instagram or video embeds are served by those platforms under their own policies.

Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking cookies. Third-party embeds may set their own.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep booking messages only as long as needed for your care. Ask us any time to delete them.

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, ask for corrections or ask for deletion. Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram.
