Brows that suit your face

Microblading adds fine hair-like strokes. Powder and ombre brows give a soft, shaded look. Before any pigment is placed, we draw the shape with you and agree it together.

Who it is best for

Sparse or uneven brows

Fill gaps and balance the shape.

Busy mornings

Wake up with brows already done.

Smudge-free colour

Stays put through heat and humidity.

How your session works

Simple, safe and unhurried.

Consultation

We talk about your goals, skin and daily routine.

Design and shade

We map the shape and match a colour to your skin tone. You approve it first.

Procedure

Numbing cream, sterile single-use needles and a calm, careful session.

Healing and touch-up

Clear aftercare steps, then a touch-up to perfect the result.

Questions about eyebrow permanent makeup

Microblading or powder brows?

Microblading suits natural, sparse brows. Powder and ombre suit oily skin or a soft makeup look. We help you choose.

How long do permanent brows last?

Usually one to three years, depending on skin and aftercare.
