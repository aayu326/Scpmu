How long does permanent makeup last?

Most PMU lasts one to three years depending on your skin, lifestyle and aftercare. A touch-up keeps the colour fresh.

Does it hurt?

A numbing cream is used before treatment, so most clients feel only mild pressure or tingling.

Is it safe?

How do I choose the best permanent makeup artist?

Look at healed results (not only fresh photos), check that single-use needles are used, and ask for a patch test and a consultation before you decide.

What is the difference between microblading, powder brows and ombre brows?

Microblading draws fine hair-like strokes and suits natural, sparse brows. Powder and ombre brows give a soft, shaded, makeup-like look. We will suggest the best style for your skin and brow shape.

Is permanent makeup the same as a tattoo?

It uses a similar idea, but permanent makeup (also called cosmetic tattoo) places pigment in the upper skin layers, with lighter colours that fade softly over time.
