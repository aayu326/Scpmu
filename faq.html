Common questions

Where is the studio?

Opening soon on Chitrwani Road, Purnea, Bihar. Message us on WhatsApp for updates.

When does the studio open?

Soon. The date will be shared on this website and on Instagram @schauhanpmu.

How long does permanent makeup last?

Most PMU lasts one to three years depending on your skin, lifestyle and aftercare.

Does it hurt?

A numbing cream is used, so most clients feel only mild pressure or tingling.

Is it safe?

Yes. We use sterile, single-use needles and a strict hygiene routine for every client.

How should I prepare?

Avoid waxing, threading or tinting your brows for a few days before, and come with a clean face.

How long does healing take?

The surface settles in one to two weeks. Colour looks softer once healed.

How much does it cost?

See our pricing page, or message us for a quote after a short consultation.

Aftercare checklist

Follow these for best healing.

Keep it clean and dry

Gently clean as advised. Do not soak the area.

Do not scratch or pick

Let flakes fall off on their own.

Avoid sun and swimming

Stay out of strong sun, pools and saunas until healed.
