Defined eyes, no smudging

A fine line along the lashes makes eyes look fuller and more awake. Choose a soft, barely-there line or a bolder look.

Who it is best for

No daily liner to smudge.

Sparse lashes

Busy schedules

How your session works

Simple, safe and unhurried.

Consultation

We talk about your goals, skin and daily routine.

Design and shade

We map the shape and match a colour to your skin tone. You approve it first.

Procedure

Numbing cream, sterile single-use needles and a calm, careful session.

Healing and touch-up

Clear aftercare steps, then a touch-up to perfect the result.

Questions about permanent eyeliner

Is it safe near the eyes?

Yes, when done by a trained artist with sterile single-use needles. We assess suitability first.

How long does it take?

Usually one to two hours including design and numbing.
